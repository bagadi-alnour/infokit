# Domain Name Candidates

## Overview

InfoKit is designed as a **generic, globally-deployable platform** for public information and community coordination. The domain should reflect this scalability and mission, not tie to a specific location.

---

## Recommended Candidates

### Primary Tier (Most aligned with mission)

| Domain              | TLD   | Notes                                               |
| ------------------- | ----- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **infokit.org**     | .org  | Toolkit for information distribution & coordination |
| **infonest.org**    | .org  | Community hub metaphor, welcoming tone              |
| **kharaghley.info** | .info | Custom branding, distinctive                        |

| directorium.info   | .info   | Directory + institutional feel           |

---

## Selection Criteria

When evaluating domains, prioritize:

- ✅ **Multilingual viability** — Works phonetically & conceptually in FR/EN/AR
- ✅ **Function over location** — No hardcoded geography (Calais is phase 1 only)
- ✅ **Scalability** — Remains relevant as platform expands to new territories
- ✅ **Accessibility** — Simple, memorable, works on low-bandwidth contexts
- ✅ **Search alignment** — Natural for people seeking "help," "information," "services"

---

## TLD Strategy

| TLD       | Best for                              | Notes                                                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **.org**  | Non-profit, institutional credibility | Strong for association partnerships & public trust     |
| **.info** | Information services, mission-aligned | Reflects core function; global recognition             |
| **.com**  | Generic                               | Less recommended — implies commercial, dilutes mission |
